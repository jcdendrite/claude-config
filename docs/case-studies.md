# Case studies

Longer-form writeups of specific design decisions in this repo, with primary-source citations. Each study is its own page under [`case-studies/`](case-studies/). Sibling to [`design-decisions.md`](design-decisions.md), which carries the shorter-form decision records — a case study is the empirical record behind one of them.

## Index

- [**Worktree enforcement: hook vs. CLAUDE.md prose**](case-studies/worktree-enforcement.md) — is the worktree-enforcement hook over-engineered relative to carefully-pruned CLAUDE.md prose? Works through each failure mode from primary sources, and when prose-only is sufficient.
- [**Review gates and the agent-babysitting problem**](case-studies/review-vs-babysitting.md) — does a workflow of review gates and reviewer agents cut the agent-babysitting cost or just relocate it? Grounded in 946 hook denials and review catches across this repo's transcript corpus.
- [**check-runner: charter scoping over command-pattern hardening**](case-studies/check-runner.md) — *(retired 2026-06-23)* charter scoping over command-pattern hardening; see also the Retirement section for residual-benefit measurement.
- [**Estimating effort by review surface, not implementation time**](case-studies/effort-estimation-review-surface.md) — does an LLM anchor effort estimates on human coding speed, and does encoding a "review surface, not hours" rule in the planning skills prevent it? Grounded in a corpus audit of 50,471 assistant text blocks.
- [**Hashline edit format: measured, declined, and why**](case-studies/hashline-edit-format.md) — should this repo replace Claude Code's built-in `Edit`/`Write` with Stencil's hashline edit format? Grounded in a 10,271-call transcript-corpus census; declined on a 0.77%-failure/0.67%-token-overhead ceiling with a numeric revisit trigger.
- [**Targeted reads: measured, adopted narrowly, and why**](case-studies/targeted-read-discipline.md) — should a "read narrow, not whole" instruction go into global guidance? Grounded in a 22,687-call `Read` census; adopted as one line after measurement showed 45.6% of reads were already targeted and the built-in `Read` tool description already carries the rule's other half, with the proposed hook and post-edit-re-read clause declined on a 0.49% measurement.
- [**Plan-mode subagent model resolution: measured, and why the pin holds**](case-studies/plan-mode-model-resolution.md) — does a subagent's `model:` pin genuinely override the parent's own model, or does `opusplan`'s Opus-while-planning default confound "plan mode breaks it" with "dispatches just inherit the parent"? A falsification test isolating Opus-anchored non-plan-mode parents resolves it: 178/178 still honored the pin outside plan mode — inside it, a corrected corpus re-scan finds 129/131 forced to Opus regardless of pin or explicit `model` param, with a controlled headless harness unable to reproduce the effect at all.
- [**Delegating the instrument, not just its execution: measured, and why the rule wasn't added**](case-studies/delegate-instrument-authoring.md) — does this repo's corpus show inline instrument-authoring concentrated in sessions that never dispatch a subagent, the signature of the split-objective failure a new `subagent-delegation` rule would target? A 622-session census finds the opposite: zero-dispatch sessions are 30.4% of sessions but only 6.6% of authored-payload mass. No rule ships; recorded as a rejected lever with a numeric revisit trigger.
- [**Did dropping the handoff-nudge cap from 360,000 to 150,000 actually help?**](case-studies/handoff-threshold-impact.md) — a four-tier before/after instrument battery, grounded in this machine's transcript corpus across all six configured accounts. The mechanism-engagement gate clears robustly (no sign flip across four tested cap values), but the primary cost outcome doesn't show a clean win — a mediator instrument finds handoff overhead rose monotonically alongside it. Recorded as a real trade, not a win or a regression, with a numeric revisit trigger.
- [**Front-loading Opus into authoring: does it cut review rounds?**](case-studies/opus-frontload-review-rounds.md) — a pre-registered, gated retrospective screen (186-row ledger, `changed_files`-stratified, 2,000-resample bootstrap) asking whether earlier Opus authoring reduces `/code-review` rounds. No stratum clears the adopt bar, and the one arm that could supply a reject reading is blocked by an instrument limitation (`subagent-mix` redacting `subagent_type` under this machine's unconditional multi-root scan) rather than resolved either way. Recorded as inconclusive with a named identification bound, not a negative result.
- [**Zero-segment `**/` glob match: measured, not sourced**](case-studies/claude-md-glob-zero-segment.md) — does a `**/`-led `paths:` glob also match a root-level file with zero intermediate segments? `code.claude.com/docs/en/memory` doesn't say. Five one-shot `-p`-mode sessions instrumented with `InstructionsLoaded` settle it directly: a repo-root `install.sh` read against a `paths` list with no bare-basename entry still loads, replicated on a second file. Not shown to transfer to an ordinary interactive session.
