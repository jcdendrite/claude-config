## Severity rubric (restated for this repo)

This is a dev-tooling repo stowed to every install, not a SaaS product with paying users — the source playbook's rubric is adapted accordingly rather than applied verbatim:

| Level | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **Critical** | Gate/guard fails open on all or nearly all installs, or causes irrecoverable data loss/full loss of the tool |
| **High** | A serious defect that will predictably break a core capability or cause a security-relevant failure under foreseeable conditions (a specific but common platform, a specific but common workflow) — short of "all or nearly all installs" |
| **Medium** | Bites only under specific conditions (a particular code path, a non-default opt-in, a motivated look) but the risk is material |
| **Low** | Hygiene or minor-correctness gap, limited real-world impact |
| **Very Low** | Housekeeping, negligible risk |
| **N/A** | Reviewed and confirmed sound — nothing was at risk. Also used, once (I3 below), for a candidate finding that specialist review determined was not a real gap: retained rather than dropped, so the audit trail shows what was considered and ruled out |

## Category taxonomy

**S** security, **I** infrastructure, **C** correctness/code quality, **SC** scale/duplication-maintenance-burden, **D** completeness (for this repo: missing test/regression coverage for a security- or correctness-relevant behavior, or a missing/unreachable doc, or an unrouted review-skill trigger — not "missing product feature").
